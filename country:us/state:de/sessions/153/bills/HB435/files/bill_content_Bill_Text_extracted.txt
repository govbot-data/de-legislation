Wilson HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES 153rd GENERAL ASSEMBLY HOUSE BILL NO. 435 AN ACT TO AMEND TITLES 18, 29, AND 31 OF THE DELAWARE CODE RELATING TO CERTIFIED REGISTERED NURSE ANESTHETISTS. BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF DELAWARE: Section 1. Amend Chapter 23, Title 18 of the Delaware Code by making deletions as shown by strike through and insertions as shown by underline as follows: Â§ 2318. Care by advanced practice nurses. nurses; certified registered nurse anesthetists. (a) No health insurer, health service corporation or health maintenance organization shall deny benefits for eligible services when the services are rendered or performed by an advanced practice registered nurse as long as said the advanced practice registered nurse acts within the scope of practice and/or definitions pursuant to Title 24. Any contract of insurance issued or issued for delivery in this State by health insurers, health service corporations or health maintenance organizations shall not exclude advanced practice registered nurses as providers of services covered in the contract. (b) All health insurance policies, contracts, or certificates that are delivered, issued for delivery, renewed, extended, or modified in this State and that provide coverage for a health service that a certified registered nurse anesthetist is licensed to perform under Title 24 shall provide for reimbursement of the service provided by the certified registered nurse anesthetist at a reimbursement rate that is not less than the reimbursement rate paid to licensed physicians providing the same service. (c) Nothing in this section shall be construed to prohibit a health insurer, health service corporation, or health maintenance organization from establishing variable reimbursement rates based on quality or performance measures. This Act applies to all policies, contracts, or certificates issued, renewed, modified, altered, amended, or reissued after December 31, 2026. SYNOPSIS This Act prohibits individual, group, state employee, and Medicaid health plans from differentiating in the reimbursement rate of a health service based on whether the service was provided by a certified registered nurse anesthetist or by a physician.
