SYNOPSIS This Substitute for House Bill 211 creates the Innovate Delaware Program. This Program allows an Economic Development Organization to apply for a tax credit program through the Division of Small Business. This Substitute also lowers the cap on appropriations to support the tax credit program from 10 million to 3 million dollars in a calendar year.
