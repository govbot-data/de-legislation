K.Â Johnson DELAWARE STATE SENATE 153rd GENERAL ASSEMBLY SENATE JOINT RESOLUTION NO. 20 DIRECTING THE DIVISION OF MEDICAID AND MEDICAL ASSISTANCE TO EVALUATE ASSESSMENTS FOR INDIVIDUALS RECEIVING HOME AND COMMUNITY-BASED SERVICES. WHEREAS, Home and Community-Based Services (HCBS) programs promote independence, community integration, and quality of life for thousands of Delawareans while supporting family caregivers and strengthening communities throughout the State; and WHEREAS, federal Medicaid requirements require states to assess and periodically reassess individuals receiving HCBS to determine medical necessity, level of care, and the services and supports needed to thrive in the community; and WHEREAS, Delaware currently utilizes a variety of assessment processes to determine eligibility for HCBS and to evaluate the scope of services authorized for Medicaid beneficiaries, including assessments conducted by managed care organizations; and WHEREAS, federal HCBS requirements emphasize the importance of person-centered, objective, and consistent assessments to ensure that individuals receive services appropriate to meet their needs; and WHEREAS, several states, including Iowa, have implemented independent and unified assessment models in which eligibility and support needs are evaluated by entities that are separate from organizations responsible for authorizing, managing, or paying for services; and WHEREAS, the use of an independent assessment process may promote objectivity and increase public confidence in eligibility and service determinations; and WHEREAS, Delaware is committed to maintaining the highest standards of program integrity, stewardship of public funds, accountability, and compliance with federal Medicaid requirements while ensuring eligible individuals receive needed services and supports; and WHEREAS, the use of standardized assessments and independent assessing entities across HCBS populations may improve consistency, transparency, and administrative efficiency while providing a more person-centered experience for Delawareans seeking long-term services and supports. NOW, THEREFORE: BE IT RESOLVED by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the 153rd General Assembly of the State of Delaware, with approval of the Governor, that the Department of Health and Social Services shall evaluate the HCBS service assessment process, which shall include investigating the feasibility of using independent assessment tools and independent assessment entities for the determination of support needs for individuals eligible for HCBS. BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Department shall present the General Assembly with a report that shall outline the Departmentâs actions and findings from the feasibility study. BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED the report shall be submitted to the General Assembly by March 1, 2027, with copies to the Governor, the Director and the Librarian of the Division of Legislative Services, and the Director of the Delaware Public Archives. SYNOPSIS This resolution requires the Department of Health & Social Services to evaluate the feasibility of using independent assessment tools and assessors for the determination of support needs for individuals eligible for Home and Community-Based Services in the State. The Department shall present a report to the Delaware General Assembly, by March 1, 2027, on its findings. Author: Senator Poore
